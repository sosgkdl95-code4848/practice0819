# UI/UX DESIGN SYSTEM

## Palette & Atmosphere:
- **Background**: Deep Space Dark (`#0F0D15`, `#1A1625`, `#261E35`).
- **Accent Colors**: 
  - Mars Red (`#E53E3E`) - Temperature & Warnings
  - Amber/Orange (`#DD6B20`, `#F6AD55`) - TR & Coins
  - Cyan/Blue (`#00B5D8`, `#3182CE`) - Oceans & Water
  - Emerald/Green (`#10B981`, `#48BB78`) - Oxygen & Flora
  - Neon Purple/Violet (`#805AD5`, `#9F7AEA`) - Teacher Controls & Specials
- **Card Styling**: Glassmorphism (`backdrop-blur-md bg-white/10 border border-white/10`).

## Micro-Interactions:
- Interactive hover states (`hover:scale-[1.02]` or `hover:scale-105 transition-all duration-300`).
- Neon glow effects for parameter progress bars (`box-shadow: 0 0 15px ...`).
- Dynamic popup modals for teacher actions and investment confirmations.
- Responsive layout supporting large classroom projectors, interactive displays, tablets, and mobile.
